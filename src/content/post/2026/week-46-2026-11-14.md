It was a tug-of-war week for the portfolio in Week 46, ending 14 November 2026. Total portfolio value dipped by SGD 12,312 (-2.17%) as a sharp pullback in our RSU position offset solid gains across our global ETF and local Singapore retirement (SRS) holdings. Despite the weekly decline, the underlying fundamentals of the portfolio remain firmly intact as we navigate the final stretch of the year.

### What Moved the Needle This Week

- **RSU (Apple / AAPL):** Down SGD 19,345 (-15.07%) to end at SGD 109,042. A surge in US 10-year Treasury yields toward 5.1% alongside broader semiconductor industry turmoil triggered sharp technology sector profit-taking on Wall Street, putting pressure on single-stock tech holdings.
- **ETF (Broad Global Index / VWRA & S&P 500):** Up SGD 1,779 (+0.71%) to SGD 251,249. Global equities held up resiliently, with defensive sectors and broader market participation cushioning the tech sell-off.
- **SRS (Singapore Equities):** Up SGD 5,254 (+2.79%) to SGD 193,708. Domestic Singapore equities posted a strong week, supported by a rebound in Straits Times Index (STI) blue-chip constituents and local financial stocks post-Q3 earnings.
- **FX Factor (USD/SGD):** The US Dollar held relatively firm around 1.28–1.29 SGD, supported by hawkish Federal Reserve rate expectations, which provided mild currency stability for foreign-denominated holdings when converted to SGD.

### Portfolio Progress & FIRE Glidepath

- **Glidepath Status:** Total portfolio value is at SGD 555,000 against our 2026 glidepath target of SGD 515,000. This leaves us SGD 35,000 (or 7.07%) ahead of our planned timeline.
- **Understanding YTD Returns:** Year-to-date, gross net worth growth is 33.05%, while month-to-date gain stands at -1.5%. It is important to distinguish total accumulation from market performance: the 33.05% gross growth figure reflects total net worth accumulation, which includes fresh savings contributions, SRS deposits, and retained RSU vests. Subtracting those capital injections isolates our true market performance, placing our YTD net investment return at 6.88%.

### Next-Week Outlook & Plan

We will stick strictly to routine execution next week—letting automated dollar-cost averaging into global ETFs run on schedule and keeping an eye on broader market liquidity. Short-term single-stock volatility is part of the journey; maintaining a disciplined, automated process remains the most reliable strategy on the glidepath to FIRE.
