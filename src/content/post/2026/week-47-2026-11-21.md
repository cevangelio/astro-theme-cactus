# Boring Wealth Weekly — Week 47 (Ending Nov 21, 2026)

Week 47 was a bumpy ride across global financial markets, resulting in a portfolio retreat of -SGD 24,879 (-4.52%) for the week. A steep selloff across global technology and equity markets dragged down both our international ETF holdings and Singapore retirement equities, though Apple (RSU) proved slightly more resilient. Despite the November pullback, our long-term trajectory remains firm, with total net worth continuing to sit comfortably above our full-year FIRE target.

---

### What Moved the Needle This Week

- **Broad Market & Tech Pullback (ETF: -SGD 14,772 / -5.92%):** Global equity indices experienced their longest losing streak in months as rising 10-year Treasury yields (hovering near 5%) and growing market skepticism over mega-cap AI capital expenditure hit risk assets across the board.
- **Apple Relative Resilience (RSU: -SGD 2,603 / -2.42%):** While broader tech benchmarks slid into near-correction territory, Apple held up comparatively well. Investors continue to view Apple's disciplined, capital-efficient approach to AI integration and strong fiscal Q4 results as a relative safe haven during tech swings.
- **Regional Weakness & FX Impact (SRS: -SGD 7,503 / -3.89%):** Singapore equities felt the ripple effects of global risk-off sentiment. Because our total portfolio is measured in SGD, foreign exchange movements between USD and SGD also played a role in amplifying the week's net movements.

---

### Portfolio Progress & Glidepath Highlights

- **Glidepath Status:** Total net worth stands at **SGD 525,000**, keeping us **SGD 5,000 (1.12%) ahead** of our target of SGD 520,000 for the **2026 glidepath**, even after a -6.56% Month-to-Date (MTD) drop.
- **Understanding Growth — Gross vs. Net Returns:** 
  - **Gross Net Worth Growth (26.21% YTD):** This headline accumulation number includes all market movements along with our fresh savings deposits, SRS contributions, and retained RSU vests. 
  - **Net Investment Return (0.27% YTD):** Stripping away those cash additions isolates pure market performance. This distinction shows that while market gains themselves have been nearly flat this year (+0.27%), our consistent savings rate and equity vests have successfully propelled our total net worth up by 26.21% YTD.
