# Boring Wealth Weekly: Week 48 Recap

Week 48 ending November 28, 2026 brought a steady rise to our SGD-denominated portfolio. Total net worth expanded by SGD 7,627 (+1.44%) over the week, providing a quiet boost as markets navigated the Thanksgiving holiday trading session. A broad post-holiday bounce in global equities paired with a solid recovery in Singapore domestic shares helped lift valuations across all asset classes.

### What Moved the Needle This Week

* **RSU (Apple / AAPL):** Gained SGD 2,062 (+1.91%). Apple stock held up well near record levels during Thanksgiving week, as investors favored its disciplined capital expenditure strategy and strong cash return program over higher-beta artificial intelligence peers.
* **SRS (Singapore Equities):** Added SGD 4,575 (+2.52%). Local equities led asset gains this week as the Straits Times Index stabilized and rebounded from mid-November pullbacks.
* **ETF (Broad Global Index):** Increased by SGD 989 (+0.41%). Global equities captured Wall Street's holiday rally, though USD/SGD foreign exchange movements moderated total gains when translated into local currency terms.

### Portfolio Progress & FIRE Glidepath

* **Glidepath Status:** The portfolio actual value stands at SGD 540,000, keeping us ahead of our 2026 glidepath target of SGD 520,000 by SGD 15,000 (3.19% ahead of status).
* **Accumulation vs. Market Return:** Year-to-date **Gross Net Worth Growth** reached 29.37%. It is critical to note that this headline gross figure reflects overall portfolio accumulation—combining fresh capital injections, regular SRS savings, and kept RSU vests together with market movements. In contrast, our **Net Investment Return** is 2.65%, which isolates true market performance by subtracting all fresh savings deposits and retained vests.
* **Monthly Trajectory:** While this week was positive, month-to-date performance remains at -4.23% due to earlier market softness during November (asset MTD: RSU +9.85%, ETF -3.14%, SRS -5.04%; asset YTD: RSU -0.97%, ETF +5.58%, SRS +0.23%).

### Next-Week Outlook

As we move into December, the plan remains simple: stick to automated contributions, maintain steady broad-market exposure, and let long-term compounding work without trying to time year-end market moves.
